# Six-game playfeel iteration · 2026-09-18

This is an iteration on the user's six specific complaints, not a claim of parity with KartRider or KOF97. The combat data and art remain original; no commercial ROM, sprites, or music were copied.

## Changes

- **Typebound**: replace flat illustrated characters and three battle bitmaps with procedural light spirits. Cached glow stamps, elastic membranes, per-letter orbit nodes, continuous spell trails, impact-timed recoil, restrained boundary ripples. No camera shake or screen flash. Ember / Frost / Bloom change damage, enemy charge and healing. Every three clean words causes a resonance burst. A one-energy timed parry can interrupt an attack; ordinary shield still costs three. Desktop keyboard gives space back to the arena, mobile retains its virtual keys. All twenty-six letter keys remain available for typing.
- **Apex Drive**: steer using actual ground speed instead of the forward component, which previously collapsed at high sideslip and unexpectedly amplified yaw. Faster steer/torque response, progressive deep-drift arc, slip-dependent drag, progressive grip recovery. Shift-held countersteer, genuine crossing rear tyre trails, double/triple mini-turbo and cut-drift remain supported. Balanced mode retains full CSS-pixel resolution and contact shadows without an additional sun-shadow pass; detailed quality retains it.
- **Signal Strike**: add Foundry, Tidal Station and Star Hangar, for four selectable maps. Each has three sectors, its own palette, cover geometry, enemy placement and relay route. Foundry encourages two-sided flanking; Canal has long divided lanes with crossing gaps; Hangar alternates cover and close encounters. Map selection resets the retry checkpoint.
- **Moonblade**: stable world-space dead zone, sustained-direction look-ahead instead of following every reversal, grounded-floor debounce and threshold. Normal jumps and landings do not tug the horizon. Large jumps still remain framed. Removed landing camera displacement entirely.
- **Word Ranger**: add three authored operations, from three to six. Rainy Freight has two-level cargo routes and barrel ambushes; Frozen Express uses horizontal transport platforms and elevated supplies; Furnace Run alternates sunken lanes and overhead crossfire. Distinct cached backdrops and restrained weather do not obscure collision surfaces.
- **Fury**: seven-second paid MAX, connected-special super cancel, command grabs, backward roll, landing-buffered recovery without snapping to ground. Mei's two-hit rush and fast low game differ from Shan's low heavy projectile and stronger grab. CPU confirms contacts into follow-ups and mixes overhead / low / throw against passive guard. Recovery punish, MAX and super-cancel have concise audiovisual feedback.

## Automated evidence

`node --test tests/*.test.mjs english-word-ranger/tests/engine.test.mjs`: **344 passing**.

New targeted tests cover paid/nonstacking MAX, two-hit rush, grab startup/whiff, backward roll, continuous fall recovery, super-cancel stock cost, camera reversal hysteresis, tiny-step/landing stability, high-jump framing, drift speed continuity and reverse torque, spell tradeoffs, resonance and timed parry.

`tests/strike-maps.test.mjs`: all four maps have traversable relay routes. A test-only controller uses ordinary WASD, aim, fire, reload and use inputs to clear all three sectors on standard difficulty. It does not rewrite world state, health, collision, position or ammunition.

`RANGER_TEST_WIDTH=540 node english-word-ranger/tests/playthrough.mjs 0 1 2 3 4 5`: all six operations won. New operations completed in 37.36, 38.10 and 40.02 simulation seconds, with bounded bullets/particles. The wider 960-unit tests also clear all three new operations.

## Browser checks

Tests run one game at a time in Codex's built-in browser. Each harness stops the game's animation and audio on completion. Browser figures below are observations on this host, not promises for all devices.

- Typebound: 15 animation checks and 32 input/resource checks passed, including exact flight/contact timing, no duplicate damage from retyped letters, reduced motion, pause, and eight start/exit cycles. Battle image count is now zero. A sampled animation run had p95 JS/render-submission work of 0.8 ms; frame intervals also include host/GPU scheduling.
- Typebound: the complete nine-room production-input run passed all 14 checks, including four enemy kinds, sentence bosses, real wrong-key recording and final shutdown. It completed in 59.56 wall-clock seconds using accelerated ordinary typing, not health or progression writes. This validates reachability, not a typical player's completion time. p95 JS/render-submission work was 0.9 ms; the host did show some 30 Hz frame intervals, so no locked-frame-rate claim is made.
- Typebound and Fury were checked at 390 × 844 portrait and 844 × 390 landscape with touch emulation. Typebound virtual typing advanced the real word; the landscape spell bar was compacted to keep the keyboard unobstructed. All ten Fury action buttons remained in bounds and touch MAX activated the real seven-second state. Temporary emulation was reset afterwards.
- Typebound's seven additional spell checks passed: number-key selection does not type a wrong letter, click selection matches its UI, one completed word grants one charge, natural enemy charge lights the parry window, and Enter spends that one charge to interrupt and retaliate. The test ends with audio and animation stopped.
- Apex: eight drift technique checks passed, including double/triple mini-turbo, no held-key auto-spray and cut-drift. Thirteen free-drift checks passed in both directions, with sideslip above 136 degrees, actual rear-contact paths intersecting, reverse yaw torque within 120 ms, no wall assistance, and stopped audio/RAF afterwards.
- Apex: after removing the additional real-time shadow pass in balanced quality, the same free-drift check improved from median 32 ms to 16.8 ms frame interval on this host. p95 was still 33.3 ms, so this is not a claim of locked 60 fps. p95 JS/render-submission work was 1.5 ms; actual pixel resolution and the soft kart contact shadow were retained.
- Fury: 22 production-input checks passed, including MAX / command-grab key bindings, guard/low/overhead, hit confirms, local two-player isolation, pause and audio release.
- Fury: a 60-second sparring run exercised eleven move types and 51 contacts, including MAX and command grabs. Median frame interval was 16.7 ms, p95 18.3 ms; p95 JS/render-submission work 1.6 ms, no frames over 50 ms in the retained sample. Pause left no animation loop or audio voices.
- Moonblade: all 22 production-input checks passed. Four ordinary jumps also passed a 157-sample rendered-pixel check: the static roof/wall patch and camera height did not change. No jumping horizon or landing displacement.
- Signal Strike: 25 browser checks passed across two complete four-map selection/start/move/fire/exit cycles. GPU allocations were stable at 75 geometries and 22 textures after warming the four maps. Foundry and Hangar were also visually inspected in active gameplay.
- Word Ranger: Rainy Freight completed a full rendered browser playthrough in 36.42 simulation seconds, boss defeated, six HP, audio stopped on win. Median frame 16.7 ms, p95 18.5 ms, p95 JS/render work 1.4 ms at a 2037 × 1146 drawing buffer. The two-level route, rain and cargo/crane background were visually checked.
- Frozen Express and Furnace Run were visually checked in motion. The ice stage's inherited moss bitmap was found to clash with its setting and replaced by cached ice facets and a clear snow lip; the furnace's metal was given a warm copper edge. Collision geometry is unchanged.

## Reference boundary

The [official KartRider drift improvement note](https://kartdrift.nexon.com/kartdrift/ko/news/announcement/view?threadId=2748954) was used as a design reference, not as a source of hidden physics constants. [SNK's KOF97 page](https://www.snk-corp.co.jp/official/akeakaneogeo/kof97/) and [KOF2002 system description](https://www.snk-corp.co.jp/official/kof2002/english/e_kof2002_system.html) inform the distinction between neutral, guard, meter and cancel choices. The new MAX/super-cancel system is an original franchise-inspired mechanic, not an assertion that KOF97 has this exact ruleset.
